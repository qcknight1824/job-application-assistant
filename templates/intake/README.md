# Intake.

Optional starting material for `career-bootstrap`. Everything here is private.

- `sources/` — drop files here: your latest resume, LinkedIn export, performance reviews, promotion packets, brag docs, decks, harvest-prompt output. Formats: PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD. Legacy `.doc`: re-save as `.docx`.
- `harvest-prompt.md` — a prompt to run in your work AI assistant when your best evidence lives in work systems. Read its "before you run it" section first.
- `extract.md` — written by the agent: roles found, every claim with its source, conflicts, and what is missing. The interview starts from it.

Only add material your employer permits you to share. Nothing in `sources/` is quoted in external documents; resumes and cover letters draw only from the confirmed ledger in `profile/`. After bootstrap you can delete `sources/` and keep `extract.md`.
